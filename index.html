What is InterACT?

InterACT (Interactive Activity Maker) is a specialized pedagogical intervention designed to bridge the gap between traditional teaching and digital interactivity. It serves as a creative workspace where educators transform static lesson plans into dynamic, high-engagement learning experiences.

🎓

Empowering the Digital Educator

Developed under the principles of User-Centered Design (UCD), InterACT focuses on lowering technical barriers for teachers. It provides a "Zero-Code" environment, ensuring that the focus remains on effective teaching and instructional design rather than complex programming. By simplifying the creation process, it allows teachers to produce professional-grade interactive materials in a fraction of the time.

✏️

Contextualization and Localization

A core strength of InterACT is its support for Contextualized Learning. Unlike rigid, pre-made educational software, InterACT empowers teachers to integrate local culture, native languages, and specific community examples into their digital activities. This ensures that the technology resonates with the students' lived experiences and meets specific classroom needs.

🧠

Enhancing the Learning Environment

InterACT aims to reduce "technostress" for both teachers and students. By gamifying assessment and review, it lowers the students' Affective Filter (anxiety) and increases motivation through immediate feedback. It is built to be a stable, offline-capable, and reliable companion in any classroom — from high-tech laboratories to resource-limited settings.
